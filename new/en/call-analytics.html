Frequently Asked Questions

How accurate is transcription?

Our AI achieves 99.5% accuracy in transcriptions. The system continuously improves and adapts to accents, industry-specific jargon, and technical terms. Supports 20+ languages with the same quality level.

Is real-time analysis possible?

Yes! Transcription and sentiment analysis occur in real-time. Agents can see live insights during the call, and supervisors can receive instant alerts about problematic calls.

Can it integrate with my CRM?

Yes, we have native integrations with Salesforce, HubSpot, Zoho, and 50+ CRMs. We can also integrate with any system via API. Transcriptions and analysis automatically sync to customer records.

How long is data retained?

It depends on your plan: Basic (90 days), Professional (1 year), Enterprise (unlimited). You can download reports and transcriptions at any time before expiration.

Is it GDPR compliant?

Absolutely. Our platform fully complies with GDPR, CCPA, and other privacy regulations. All data is encrypted, stored in secure servers, and you maintain full control over your information.
