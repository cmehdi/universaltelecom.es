Frequently Asked Questions

Do I need to install hardware?

No. Our virtual PBX is 100% cloud-based. You only need an internet connection and our apps or IP phones.

Can I keep my current phone number?

Yes. We can port your existing numbers to our platform at no additional cost. The process takes 7-10 business days.

What happens if the internet goes down?

You can configure automatic backup routes to forward calls to mobile phones or other numbers in case of internet failure.

How many simultaneous calls can I make?

Our Starter plan includes 6 simultaneous channels. Professional includes 15 and Enterprise is unlimited.

Is technical support included?

Yes. All plans include technical support. Professional and Enterprise plans have priority 24/7 support.

Can I cancel anytime?

Yes. There are no permanence contracts. You can cancel anytime and only pay for the time used.
