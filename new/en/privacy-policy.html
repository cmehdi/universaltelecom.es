2. What Personal Data We Collect

We collect different types of personal data depending on how you interact with our services:

2.1 Data You Provide Directly

  • Account Registration: Name, email address, phone number, company name, job title, billing address
  • Service Usage: Call logs, SMS records, voicemail recordings, chat transcripts, contact lists
  • Payment Information: Credit card details, billing address, VAT number (processed securely through our payment providers)
  • Communication: Any information you provide when contacting our support team, completing surveys, or attending events
  • Technical Support: Information about technical issues, error logs, system configurations

2.2 Data Collected Automatically

  • Usage Data: IP address, browser type and version, time zone settings, browser plug-in types
  • Device Information: Device type, operating system, unique device identifiers
  • Analytics Data: Pages visited, time spent on pages, click-through rates, date and time of visit
  • Cookies and Tracking: Information collected through cookies and similar technologies (see our Cookie Policy)
  • Location Data: General location based on IP address (not precise geolocation)

2.3 Data from Third Parties

  • Business Partners: Information from authorized resellers or integration partners
  • Public Sources: Publicly available business information for B2B purposes
  • Social Media: Public profile information if you interact with us on social platforms
  • Service Providers: Data from payment processors, fraud prevention services, and marketing platforms

We process your personal data for the following purposes:

3.1 Service Delivery (Legal Basis: Contract Performance)

  • Provide and maintain our telecommunications services
  • Process your transactions and manage payments
  • Route calls, deliver SMS messages, and enable communication features

3.2 Service Improvement (Legal Basis: Legitimate Interest)

  • Analyze usage patterns to improve our services

3.3 Security and Fraud Prevention (Legal Basis: Legitimate Interest / Legal Obligation)

  • Detect and prevent fraudulent activities
  • Ensure platform security and integrity
  • Comply with legal obligations and regulatory requirements
  • Investigate and resolve disputes
  • Protect against unauthorized access

3.4 Marketing and Communication (Legal Basis: Consent / Legitimate Interest)

  • Send you marketing communications about our products and services (with your consent)
  • Provide personalized recommendations
  • Invite you to events, webinars, and promotions
  • Conduct surveys and market research

3.5 Legal Compliance (Legal Basis: Legal Obligation)

  • Comply with telecommunications regulations
  • Respond to legal requests and court orders
  • Maintain records as required by law
  • Report to regulatory authorities when required

4. Legal Basis for Processing

Under GDPR, we process your personal data based on one or more of the following legal grounds:

Contract Performance: Processing is necessary to fulfill our contractual obligations to you

Legal Obligation: Processing is required to comply with applicable laws and regulations

Legitimate Interest: Processing is necessary for our legitimate business interests, such as fraud prevention, service improvement, and direct marketing (when not requiring consent)

Consent: You have given explicit consent for specific processing activities, such as marketing communications

Vital Interest: Processing is necessary to protect someone's life or physical integrity

6. International Data Transfers

Your personal data may be transferred to and processed in countries outside the European Economic Area (EEA). When we transfer your data internationally, we ensure appropriate safeguards are in place:

  • Adequacy Decisions: We transfer data to countries recognized by the European Commission as providing adequate data protection
  • Standard Contractual Clauses: We use EU-approved Standard Contractual Clauses (SCCs) with service providers in countries without adequacy decisions
  • Binding Corporate Rules: Some of our service providers have Binding Corporate Rules approved by EU data protection authorities
  • Privacy Shield (where applicable): For US-based processors, we ensure compliance with applicable data transfer mechanisms

For more information about our data transfer safeguards, please contact our Data Protection Officer at dpo@universaltelecom.com.

8. Your Data Protection Rights

Under GDPR and other data protection laws, you have the following rights regarding your personal data:

8.1 Right of Access

You have the right to request a copy of the personal data we hold about you. You can access most of your data through your account dashboard or by contacting us.

8.2 Right to Rectification

You have the right to request correction of inaccurate or incomplete personal data. You can update most information directly in your account settings.

8.3 Right to Erasure ("Right to be Forgotten")

You have the right to request deletion of your personal data when:

  • The data is no longer necessary for the purposes it was collected
  • You withdraw consent and there is no other legal ground for processing
  • You object to processing and there are no overriding legitimate grounds
  • The data has been unlawfully processed
  • The data must be erased to comply with a legal obligation

8.4 Right to Restriction of Processing

You have the right to request restriction of processing when:

  • You contest the accuracy of the data
  • The processing is unlawful but you don't want erasure
  • We no longer need the data but you need it for legal claims
  • You have objected to processing pending verification of legitimate grounds

8.5 Right to Data Portability

You have the right to receive your personal data in a structured, commonly used, and machine-readable format and to transmit it to another controller when technically feasible.

8.6 Right to Object

You have the right to object to processing based on legitimate interests or for direct marketing purposes. We will cease processing unless we can demonstrate compelling legitimate grounds.

8.7 Right to Withdraw Consent

When processing is based on consent, you have the right to withdraw consent at any time. This will not affect the lawfulness of processing before withdrawal.

8.8 Right to Lodge a Complaint

You have the right to lodge a complaint with your local data protection authority if you believe your rights have been violated. In Spain, this is the Agencia Española de Protección de Datos (AEPD): www.aepd.es

8.9 Automated Decision-Making

You have the right not to be subject to decisions based solely on automated processing, including profiling, which produce legal effects or similarly significantly affect you.

9. Security Measures

We implement appropriate technical and organizational measures to protect your personal data:

9.1 Technical Measures

  • Encryption: All data in transit is encrypted using TLS 1.3. Data at rest is encrypted using AES-256
  • Access Controls: Multi-factor authentication, role-based access control, principle of least privilege
  • Network Security: Firewalls, intrusion detection systems, DDoS protection
  • Monitoring: 24/7 security monitoring, automated threat detection, regular security audits
  • Secure Development: Security-by-design principles, regular code reviews, vulnerability scanning

9.2 Organizational Measures

  • Staff Training: Regular data protection and security training for all employees
  • Confidentiality: All staff are bound by confidentiality obligations
  • Vendor Management: Due diligence on all third-party processors, contractual data protection obligations
  • Incident Response: Data breach response plan, incident management procedures
  • Business Continuity: Regular backups, disaster recovery plans, redundant systems

9.3 Certifications and Compliance

  • ISO 27001 (Information Security Management)
  • SOC 2 Type II (Service Organization Controls)
  • GDPR Compliance
  • PCI DSS (for payment card data)
  • Regular third-party security audits

Data Breach Notification: In the event of a data breach, we will notify affected users and relevant authorities within 72 hours as required by GDPR.
