Frequently Asked Questions

How long does it take to activate a number?

Most numbers activate in less than 5 minutes. Some countries may require documentation and take 1-3 business days. We'll inform you of specific requirements before purchase.

Can I port my existing number?

Yes! We support number porting in most countries. The process takes 7-15 business days depending on the country and current provider. We handle the entire process.

Can I change the call destination?

Absolutely. You can change the forwarding destination at any time from the dashboard. Changes take effect immediately. No additional charges.

Are there setup fees?

No, we don't charge setup fees on any number. You only pay the monthly rental and usage according to the published rates.

Can I send and receive SMS?

Yes, most of our numbers support two-way SMS. During purchase, we'll indicate if a specific number has SMS capability. SMS rates apply per message sent or received.

What happens if I cancel?

You can cancel at any time without penalties. We'll prorate the unused time. If you decide to return, you can reactivate the number (subject to availability) or get a new one.
