Frequently Asked Questions

What's the difference between WhatsApp Business and WhatsApp Business API?

WhatsApp Business is the free app for small businesses. The API is for medium to large businesses needing multi-agent support, automations and integrations.

Can I keep my current WhatsApp number?

Yes, but you'll need a new number for the API as regular WhatsApp and the API can't use the same number simultaneously.

How long does verification take?

WhatsApp verification typically takes 1-3 business days. We'll handle the entire process for you.

Can I send promotional messages?

Yes, but only to customers who have opted in. All messages must follow WhatsApp policies to avoid blocking.

What is a conversation?

A conversation is a 24-hour window from the first message. You can send unlimited messages during that window.

Is technical support included?

Yes. All plans include technical support. Professional and Enterprise plans have priority 24/7 support.
