Frequently Asked Questions

How natural do AI voice agents sound?

Our Utio Voice AI agents use the latest natural language generation technology. Most customers can't distinguish them from a human agent. The AI understands context, uses natural pauses, and adapts to conversation tone.

What happens if the AI can't resolve a query?

The agent automatically detects when it needs human help and smoothly transfers the call to your team, providing full context of the conversation. You can also configure specific situations for automatic escalation.

How long does implementation take?

Most clients have their AI agent operational in less than 48 hours. Configuration is intuitive and doesn't require technical knowledge. Our team assists you throughout the process.

Can I customize the agent's personality?

Yes, completely. You can define tone (formal, casual, friendly), language style, specific responses, and even create custom voices. The agent adapts to your brand.

What languages are supported?

We support 20+ languages including English, Spanish, French, German, Italian, Portuguese, Chinese, Japanese, and many more. The agent automatically detects the customer's language.

Is there a free trial?

Yes! We offer a 30-day free trial with no credit card required. Includes 500 minutes and access to all features. Cancel anytime without commitment.
