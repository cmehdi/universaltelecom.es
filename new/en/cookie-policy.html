Cookie Policy

Last updated: January 2024

This Cookie Policy explains how Universal Telecom uses cookies and similar tracking technologies on our website. It provides information about what cookies are, how we use them, and how you can control them.

1. What Are Cookies?

Cookies are small text files that are placed on your device (computer, tablet, or mobile phone) when you visit a website. They are widely used to make websites work more efficiently and provide information to website owners.

Key Characteristics of Cookies:

  • Cookies contain small amounts of information
  • They are stored on your device's hard drive
  • They help websites remember information about your visit
  • They can improve your browsing experience
  • They allow websites to recognize you when you return

2. Types of Cookies We Use

2.1 By Purpose

🔧 Essential Cookies (Strictly Necessary)

These cookies are essential for the website to function properly. They enable core functionality such as security, network management, and accessibility. You cannot opt-out of these cookies.

Examples: Session management, authentication, load balancing

⚙️ Preference Cookies (Functional)

These cookies allow the website to remember choices you make (such as language, region, or theme) and provide enhanced, more personalized features.

Examples: Language preferences, theme settings, form data

📊 Analytics Cookies (Performance)

These cookies help us understand how visitors interact with our website by collecting and reporting information anonymously. This helps us improve the website's performance.

Examples: Google Analytics, visitor counts, page views, bounce rates

🎯 Marketing Cookies (Targeting/Advertising)

These cookies track your browsing habits to deliver advertising that is more relevant to you and your interests. They may also limit the number of times you see an advertisement.

Examples: Google Ads, Facebook Pixel, LinkedIn Insight Tag

2.2 By Duration

Session Cookies: These are temporary cookies that expire when you close your browser. They help websites remember what you do from page to page during a single browsing session.

Persistent Cookies: These cookies remain on your device for a set period (which can vary from a few hours to several years) and are activated each time you visit the website that created them.

2.3 By Origin

First-Party Cookies: These are set by the website you are visiting (universaltelecom.com). Only we can read these cookies.

Third-Party Cookies: These are set by a domain other than the one you are visiting. They are commonly used for advertising and analytics purposes.

4. How to Manage Cookies

You have several options to manage and control cookies:

4.1 Cookie Banner

When you first visit our website, you will see a cookie banner that allows you to accept or customize your cookie preferences. You can:

  • Accept all cookies by clicking "Accept All"
  • Customize your preferences by clicking "Cookie Settings"
  • Change your preferences at any time using the cookie icon in the bottom corner

4.2 Browser Settings

Most web browsers allow you to control cookies through their settings. Here's how to manage cookies in popular browsers:

🌐 Google Chrome

  1. Click the three dots menu (⋮) in the top right corner
  2. Select "Settings"
  3. Click "Privacy and security" in the left sidebar
  4. Click "Cookies and other site data"
  5. Choose your preferred cookie settings

Learn more about Chrome cookies

🦊 Mozilla Firefox

  1. Click the menu button (☰) in the top right corner
  2. Select "Settings"
  3. Click "Privacy & Security" in the left sidebar
  4. Under "Cookies and Site Data", choose your preferences
  5. You can also click "Manage Data" to view and delete specific cookies

Learn more about Firefox cookies

🧭 Safari

  1. Click "Safari" in the menu bar
  2. Select "Preferences"
  3. Click the "Privacy" tab
  4. Choose your cookie preferences under "Cookies and website data"
  5. Click "Manage Website Data" to view and remove specific cookies

Learn more about Safari cookies

🌊 Microsoft Edge

  1. Click the three dots menu (⋯) in the top right corner
  2. Select "Settings"
  3. Click "Cookies and site permissions" in the left sidebar
  4. Click "Cookies and site data"
  5. Configure your cookie preferences

Learn more about Edge cookies

4.3 Mobile Devices

iOS (Safari):

  1. Go to Settings > Safari
  2. Scroll down to "Privacy & Security"
  3. Toggle "Block All Cookies" or "Prevent Cross-Site Tracking"

Android (Chrome):

  1. Open Chrome app
  2. Tap the three dots menu (⋮)
  3. Go to Settings > Site settings > Cookies
  4. Choose your preferred cookie settings

4.4 Third-Party Opt-Out Tools

You can also use third-party tools to manage cookies and online tracking:

  • Your Online Choices (EU)
  • Digital Advertising Alliance (US)
  • Digital Advertising Alliance of Canada
  • Google Analytics Opt-out Browser Add-on

5. Impact of Disabling Cookies

While you can disable cookies, please note that doing so may affect your experience on our website:

If you disable essential cookies:

  • You may not be able to log in to your account
  • Some features of the website may not function properly
  • Your security may be compromised

If you disable preference cookies:

  • Your language and theme preferences will not be saved
  • You will need to reconfigure settings each time you visit

If you disable analytics cookies:

  • We will not be able to improve the website based on user behavior
  • Performance issues may take longer to identify and fix

If you disable marketing cookies:

  • You may still see advertisements, but they will be less relevant
  • You may see the same ads repeatedly

6. Other Tracking Technologies

In addition to cookies, we may use other tracking technologies:

6.1 Web Beacons (Pixel Tags)

Web beacons are tiny graphics with a unique identifier, similar in function to cookies. They are used to track online movements of web users and to understand which emails have been opened and acted upon. Unlike cookies, web beacons are embedded invisibly on web pages or in emails.

6.2 Local Storage

HTML5 local storage allows websites to store data locally on your device. We use local storage to save preferences and improve performance. You can clear local storage through your browser settings.

6.3 Session Storage

Session storage is similar to local storage but is cleared when you close your browser. We use it to maintain state during your browsing session.

6.4 Device Fingerprinting

We may collect information about your device (browser type, operating system, screen resolution) to create a unique "fingerprint" for security and fraud prevention purposes.

7. Do Not Track (DNT)

Some browsers include a "Do Not Track" (DNT) feature that signals to websites that you do not want to have your online activity tracked. Currently, there is no industry-wide standard for recognizing or responding to DNT signals.

At present, our website does not respond to DNT signals. However, you can still control cookies and tracking through the methods described in Section 4 of this policy.
